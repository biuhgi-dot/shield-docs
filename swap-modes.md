# Swap modes

## Regular

One exchange. The trail is broken once. Fastest and cheapest; most swaps finish in a few minutes.

## Privacy

Two exchanges on two networks. Your coins go to exchange A, which pays exchange B on another network, which pays you. No single exchange sees both your sending and your receiving address. Slower and costs a little more, since two exchanges each take their spread.

## Split

One total, many receiving addresses, up to 20. Shield opens one exchange order per address and can make the amounts uneven (equal, or each within 25% or 50% of its share) so the pieces do not match. You make one deposit per address.

## Private Split

Split, and every part goes through two exchanges on two networks. Up to 10 addresses, since each part is two exchange orders.

## Which to pick

Regular for speed and price. Privacy when you want no exchange to see both ends. Split to seed several wallets from one pot. Private Split for both.
