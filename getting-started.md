# Getting started

Shield swaps one coin for another between networks, privately, at the cheapest price it can find. You can use it on the website, [shieldswaps.xyz](https://shieldswaps.xyz), or in Telegram with the Shield bot. Both ask the same questions in the same order.

## What you need

- Coins in a wallet you control, on one of the networks Shield supports (Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Robinhood Chain, Solana, BNB Chain, Polygon, Avalanche, Bitcoin, Tron, TON, Litecoin, Dogecoin, XRP Ledger).

- A receiving address on the network you want the coins to arrive on. For XRP, the destination tag as well.

- No account, no email, no identification. The first time, you agree to the [terms of use](terms-of-use.md) once.

## The eleven steps

1. Choose a mode: Regular, Privacy, Split or Private Split.

2. The network you are sending from.

3. The coin you are sending.

4. How much, in coin or in dollars.

5. The network you want to receive on.

6. The coin you want to receive.

7. Your receiving address (or addresses, in a Split).

8. Shield asks every exchange it can reach for a price and opens your swap at the cheapest.

9. Send exactly the amount shown to the deposit address, within 90 minutes.

10. Watch it move: waiting, confirming, exchanging, sending.

11. Complete. Keep the receipt.

The bar at the top fills as you go.
