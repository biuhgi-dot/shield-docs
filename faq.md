# FAQ

## Is there a minimum?

Each exchange has its own. Shield tells you on the amount step if yours is too small for every route.

## How long does a swap take?

Regular swaps usually finish in a few minutes after the deposit confirms. Privacy swaps take two hops, so longer. Bitcoin confirmations take longest.

## I sent a different amount.

The exchange processes what it received at the rate at that moment. Much less may fall under its minimum; then it refunds through its own order page.

## I sent the wrong coin or the wrong network.

That can lose the swap. Contact the exchange with the order id at once; recovery is at its discretion and may cost a fee. Shield cannot recover it.

## Which exchange handled my swap?

Shield does not headline it while all is well. It is named, with a link to its order page, whenever you need to deal with it: paused, refunding, failed. The receipt carries the exchange order id.

## Will I be asked for ID?

Never by Shield. An exchange can pause a flagged deposit and ask. That conversation is with the exchange.

## What does Shield charge?

A small fee inside the price you are shown, paid to Shield by the exchange. Nothing is added afterwards.

## Can I cancel?

Before you pay, yes: just do not send, and the order closes after 90 minutes. After you pay, the exchange is processing it and it cannot be stopped.

## Why does Split ask for several deposits?

Each address is its own exchange order, paid out from the exchange's wallet, and nobody holds the pot in between.

## Can I type dollars?

Yes. The amount step takes `$50` and converts at the current price. The order is placed in coin.

## I closed the page. How do I find my order?

With the Shield order id: Track an order on the site, or `/track` in the bot. The exchange order id works on the exchange's own page.
