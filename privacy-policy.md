# Privacy policy

_Last updated 12 October 2026. This policy covers the Shield website and the Shield Telegram bot._

## 1. What Shield collects

Shield has no accounts and collects no personal data. There is no sign-up, no email, no name, no identification, no cookies for tracking, no analytics and no advertising. To make a swap you give Shield the addresses and amounts the swap needs, and nothing else.

## 2. What an order record holds

While a swap is being tracked Shield keeps: the Shield order id, the exchange and its order id, the deposit address the exchange returned, your receiving address, the amounts, the last status and transaction hashes from the exchange, and the time the order was opened. In Telegram, the chat id is kept only while that chat is tracking an order, so the tracking message can be updated. No IP address, device information, user agent, Telegram user name or other identifier is kept.

## 3. How long

An open order is kept until it finishes, and for at most 7 days. A finished, refunded, failed or expired order is deleted 24 hours after it ended. Quotes that did not become orders are not kept at all. There are no backups of order data. Server logs carry no order details.

## 4. Who it is shared with

The exchange that processes your swap receives your receiving address and the amounts, and keeps its own records under its own privacy terms ([SideShift](https://sideshift.ai/legal), [ChangeNOW](https://changenow.io/terms-of-use/changenow-terms), [Exolix](https://exolix.com/terms)). Telegram carries what you type to the bot under Telegram's terms; Telegram chats with bots are not end-to-end encrypted. Shield shares information with no one else, except where a valid legal request requires it.

## 5. On your device

The website stores one flag in your browser to remember that you agreed to the terms. Nothing else is stored on your device.

## 6. Your rights

Because nothing identifies you, there is nothing to look up by name. If you want an order record deleted before its 24-hour or 7-day limit, email [shieldswaps@protonmail.com](mailto:shieldswaps@protonmail.com) with the Shield order id.

## 7. Security

Order records live on Shield's server in a file readable only by the service, written over an encrypted connection and served only over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; Shield keeps as little as possible for as short as possible so that a breach would expose as little as possible.

## 8. Changes

This policy can change. The date at the top tells you when. Using Shield after a change means you accept the changed policy.
