# Contact

For a question about an order, have the Shield order id ready. It is on the deposit screen, in the order link and on the receipt.

- Email: [shieldswaps@protonmail.com](mailto:shieldswaps@protonmail.com)

- Order tracking: Track an order on the site, or `/track` in the bot.

- A paused, refunding or failed swap: the exchange's order page, linked from the status screen, is where it is resolved.

Never share your wallet's recovery phrase or secret with anyone. Shield will never ask for it.
