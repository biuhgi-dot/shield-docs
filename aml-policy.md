# AML and compliance policy

_Last updated 12 October 2026._

## 1. What Shield is

Shield compares prices from independent cryptocurrency exchanges and opens swaps at the cheapest. Shield is not an exchange, a custodian or a money transmitter: it never holds, receives or forwards funds. Every swap is carried out by a licensed or registered exchange under that exchange's own anti-money-laundering programme.

## 2. Prohibited use

Shield must not be used to launder money, to finance terrorism, to evade sanctions or taxes, to defraud anyone, or to move the proceeds of crime. By using Shield you confirm that your funds come from lawful sources and that you are not a sanctioned person or located in a sanctioned place.

## 3. Screening

Shield performs no identity checks. The exchanges screen every deposit with their own monitoring and their own blocklists. A flagged deposit is paused by the exchange, which may ask for identification or the source of funds, return the funds, or hold them pending its review. Shield names the exchange and links its order page so that you can deal with it directly.

## 4. What Shield does

Shield may refuse, limit or stop serving any person, region, network or transaction at any time. Shield cooperates with valid legal requests from the authorities and may report activity it believes to be unlawful. Shield keeps order records for at most 7 days (24 hours after a swap ends), as set out in the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md); beyond that, the exchanges hold the records.

## 5. Partners

Shield routes only to exchanges that run their own compliance programmes and publish their terms. An exchange that stops doing so is removed from the route list.

## 6. Consequences

Use of Shield in breach of this policy can lead to refusal of service and to a report to the relevant authorities. Funds held by an exchange under its compliance review are released, returned or retained by that exchange under its own terms.

## 7. Changes

This policy can change. The date at the top tells you when.
